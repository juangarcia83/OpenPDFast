# F1 results — 2026-09-25

Hardware: [`bench/HARDWARE.md`](../HARDWARE.md) (Ryzen 5 3600, Windows 11, WebView2 153, 100 Hz display, DPR 1).
Background load from other desktop apps was present. Corpus: `cargo run -p corpusgen --release`.

## `cargo bench -p render` (release, `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=4`)

| Benchmark | paper-60 | big-1000p (123 MB) | plan-a0-layers |
|---|---|---|---|
| `open` (structure + all page boxes) | 0.85 ms | 21.5 ms | 0.32 ms |
| `first_page` (open + display list + all tiles at ~1200 px wide) | 10.8 ms | 31.4 ms | 7.18 s |
| `tiles/serial` at level 2 | 1 940 tiles/s | 1 820 tiles/s | 6.6 tiles/s |
| `tiles/parallel` at level 2 | 1 650 tiles/s | 1 650 tiles/s | 5.9 tiles/s |

Parallel is not faster on these pages: small text pages are dominated by per-tile overhead and glyph-cache
locks, and the plan hits the whole-list traversal described in ADR 0002. Both are F2 work.

## Real-world corpus (`pnpm corpus`), same machine

| Benchmark | pgfmanual (1324 p.) | US Topo map (1 p., 30 layers) | Darwin scan (568 p.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `open` | 302 ms | 0.68 ms | 7.8 ms |
| `first_page` | 362 ms | 1.80 s | 2.27 s |

Findings, to address next:

- **Opening pgfmanual exceeds the 150 ms budget.** Reading page boxes was 386 ms because `fz_load_page`
  also parses annotations and links; reading them from the page tree (`Document::page_size_fast`) cut that
  to 1.8 ms. The remaining ~263 ms is MuPDF building its full page-tree map on the first page lookup
  (1324 page objects spread over 651 object streams). Fix: disable the map
  (`pdf_set_page_tree_cache`), size the first pages lazily, fill the rest in the background and relayout
  without scroll jumps.
- **The map and the scan are slow to render the first page** (1.8 s and 2.3 s): vector density on the
  map (ADR 0002) and, on the scan, large JPEG 2000 images decoded per tile. Both belong to F2.

## F2 progress 2026-09-26: cell sub-lists (`renderer_tiles`, 4 threads, level 2)

| Document | cold (prepare + extract cells) | warm |
|---|---|---|
| paper-60 (light page, no cells) | 1 541 tiles/s | 1 689 tiles/s |
| pgfmanual page 1 (heavy) | 475 tiles/s | 687 tiles/s |
| USGS map (heavy) | 69 tiles/s | 135 tiles/s |

The synthetic plan barely improves: its 5 000 labels are one text node covering the whole page, copied into
every cell (ADR 0002). Splitting text nodes per cell is the next F2 task.
